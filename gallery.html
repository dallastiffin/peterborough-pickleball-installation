What You Are Looking At

A Court Is Mostly What You Cannot See

The finished court photos are the fun ones. The construction photos are the important ones.

Excavation depth, base compaction, drainage and slope decide whether a court is still flat and crack free in fifteen years. Surfacing and striping decide how it looks on day one.

When you compare quotes, ask each builder what they are doing below the slab. That is where the difference in price usually is, and it is where the difference in lifespan definitely is.
