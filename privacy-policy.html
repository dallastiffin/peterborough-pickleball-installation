Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Template notice

This is a general template. Have it reviewed against Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and your own business practices before you publish the site.

Information we collect

When you submit the quote request form on this site, we collect the information you enter: your name, email address, phone number, city or township, project type and any message you write.

We may also collect basic technical information such as your browser type, device type and the pages you visited, through standard web analytics if analytics is enabled on this site.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a site visit
  • To prepare and send you an estimate
  • To communicate with you about a project you have asked us about
  • To keep records of work we have quoted or performed

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Who we share it with

We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate, such as a form handling service, an email provider or a website host. These providers only receive what they need in order to provide their service.

We may also disclose information where we are required to do so by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain reasonable business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If web analytics is enabled, it may use cookies or similar technology to understand how visitors use the site. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details on the contact page and we will respond.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive personal or financial information through the website form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version always appears on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
